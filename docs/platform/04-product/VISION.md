# Product Vision

## The Problem

A group trip usually ends up spread across:

- WhatsApp
- Excel / Google Sheets
- Surveys
- Nequi / Daviplata / banks
- Expense-splitting apps
- Bookings
- Calendars
- Notes
- Screenshots
- Manual reminders

Coordinating money, decisions, and bookings through multiple apps is painful and error-prone.

## The Concept

One space where a group decides **what to do**, **how much it costs**, **who participates**,
**how it is funded**, and **how it is paid**.

We do NOT build an "Expense Splitter." We build a:

**Group Travel Operating System.**

## The Vision

Any group goes from:

> "We should do a trip."

to:

> "The trip is organized, funded, and ready."

without juggling money, decisions, and reservations across multiple applications.

## Core Value Proposition — 5 problems solved

1. **Decide** — Where, when, who can, what activities
2. **Plan** — What we need, how much it costs, what to book
3. **Fund** — Who contributes, how much, how often, what's missing
4. **Execute** — Bookings, payments, reminders, confirmations
5. **Control** — What's spent, what's left, who's pending, what's paid

## Differentiators

1. **Fund as the heart** — per-trip financial goal with contribution plans, automatic
   contributions, and progress tracking. This is the financial engine.
2. **Decision Engine** — polls that become Trip Configuration (Poll → Result → Decision → Config).
3. **Ledger discipline** — expected vs collected vs pending vs failed, always separated.
4. **Financial first** — organization + money first; social layer deferred.

## North Star

`Funded Trips` — trips with participants, a budget, a financial goal, and at least one
real contribution.

Each trip brings new users → reduces CAC via the product flywheel.
