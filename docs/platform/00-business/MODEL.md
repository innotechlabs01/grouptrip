# Business Model

## Revenue Streams (4 candidates)

### 1. Premium
- Free tier + Premium subscription
- Premium unlocks advanced features (AI assistant, larger groups, advanced analytics)

### 2. Transaction fee
- Small fee on certain payments processed through the platform

### 3. Provider commission
- Hotels / tours / experiences
- Flow: Booking → Provider → Commission → Platform

### 4. B2B — Trip Management SaaS
- Agencies create group trips for their clients
- Target: agencies, companies, sports groups, universities, communities, clubs, organizers

## Go-To-Market

Do NOT compete head-on with Booking, Airbnb, or tour platforms.

**Entry wedge: Friends & Family Trips.**

The initial user is "the friend who always ends up organizing the trip."
They have a real, recurring problem.

## Product Flywheel

CREATE TRIP → INVITE FRIENDS → MORE USERS → CREATE FUND → PAYMENTS → BOOK SERVICES
→ PROVIDER REVENUE → BETTER EXPERIENCE → MORE TRIPS

Each trip brings new users → CAC drops when the product is well designed.

## Strategic Decision: Coordination vs Custody

- **MVP**: Payment orchestration, not a self-owned wallet. (A — coordination)
- Avoids heavy regulatory obligations at launch.
- Custody model (B) is a future, deliberate, regulated move.
