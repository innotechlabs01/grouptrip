# Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Core Platform
- 🟢 Trip (create, invite, participants)
- 🟢 Members
- 🟢 Decisions (polls, votes, results)
- 🟢 Budget (categories, expenses)
- 🟢 Fund (goal, contributions, progress)
- 🟢 Expenses
- 🟢 Payments

## Phase 2 — Travel
- 🏨 Reservations
- ✈️ Transportation
- 🚐 Transfers
- 🎟️ Activities
- 📅 Itinerary

## Phase 3 — Intelligence
- 🤖 AI Trip Planner
- 🤖 AI Budget Advisor
- 🤖 AI recommendations
- 🤖 Automatic optimization

## Phase 4 — Marketplace
- Providers: Hotels, Tours, Transport, Restaurants, Experiences

## Phase 5 — Platform (B2B)
- Agency: Create Trips → Invite Customers → Collect Payments → Manage Reservations → Analytics

## MVP = Phase 1
Organization + money first. Social layer deferred to post-MVP.
